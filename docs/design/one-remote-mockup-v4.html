One Remote: mockup v4 (all flows)

One device, one card, one remote. How the app talks to a device (Wi-Fi, Matter or IR) is the app's problem, not a kind of device you pick. An AC is an AC: it gets the same screen whether it is reached over Matter or IR, and the app uses the best link that works right now.

First run

No account, no sign-in, no permissions up front. Permissions are asked only when a step needs them.

Add a TV

Pick TV → brand → the app finds it on Wi-Fi → you accept once on the TV → name and room. v1 brands: LG (webOS).

Add an AC

Pick AC → brand → if it has the Matter logo, scan its code; otherwise (or as well) the app finds the right IR remote by testing buttons. You are never asked "Matter or IR?".

Add a fan

The same steps as the AC: brand → (Matter if the brand has it) → IR test → name and room. The IR test for a fan presses Speed 3, then Light if the fan has one.

Device settings

From the ⋯ on a remote screen, or a long-press on a Home card.

When something is wrong

Never a spinner that hangs. Each problem says what happened and the one thing to do, and IR steps in where it can.

Decided in v1 review
  • TV: touchpad by default, D-pad on choice (like the Google TV app).
  • AC: dial with + and − buttons.
  • Home: grouped by room.
  • One device, one card, one screen. The link (Wi-Fi, Matter, IR) is not a device type and is not shown in the main UI. On phones without IR, devices that only take IR are greyed out with the reason.
  • A device can have more than one link. A Matter AC that also has an IR remote keeps IR as a quiet backup, used when Matter or Wi-Fi can't be reached.
