One Remote: first mockup

Five screens: the device list, the TV (touchpad and D-pad), the AC and a fan. Rough wireframes to react to, not final visuals. The TV screen follows the Google TV phone remote: a big touchpad or D-pad and three buttons.

Open questions for this mockup
  • TV: touchpad by default (Google style), or D-pad by default? And is Input in the ⋮ menu enough, or does it need a button?
  • TV voice: Android TV takes the phone's mic audio over the local network, and its own Assistant answers. LG and Samsung have no local voice API, so there the mic dictates: the phone turns speech into text on the device and types it into the TV's open search box (see docs/research/2026-11-02-tv-voice.md). Decided: hold to talk; with no box open, the app asks you to open one; if the phone can't do speech-to-text offline, the mic is hidden for LG and Samsung.
  • AC: dial, or a large number with + and − buttons only?
  • Home: grouped by room, or a flat list of devices?
  • Phones without IR: hide the AC and fan cards, or show them greyed out with a short explanation?
