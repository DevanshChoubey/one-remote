One Remote: first mockup

Four screens: the device list, the TV, the AC and a fan. Rough wireframes to react to, not final visuals. The TV screen follows the Google TV phone remote: a big touchpad and a handful of buttons.

Open questions for this mockup
  • TV: touchpad by default (Google style), or D-pad by default?
  • AC: dial, or a large number with + and − buttons only?
  • Home: grouped by room, or a flat list of devices?
  • Phones without IR: hide the AC and fan cards, or show them greyed out with a short explanation?
