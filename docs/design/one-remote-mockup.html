One Remote mock

The production-grade reference for every screen and state. One device, one card, one remote: how the app reaches a device (Wi-Fi, Matter, IR or Bluetooth) is the app's problem, not something you pick. Every phone below is 390 by 844, uses the app's tokens (src/theme/tokens.ts) and the copy in src/i18n/en, and most of them respond to taps.

Everything animates from the motion tokens in the CSS. Use the controls to switch theme, force reduced motion, or replay any phone.

Screen index

Every screen of the app and every state it can be in, read from src/screens, the status types in src/device, src/screens/tv/tvMode.ts and src/voice, and the copy in src/i18n/en (including the Panasonic lane's AC, fan, IR and Matter screens). Each row links to the phone that draws it; "New" marks what was added when this index was written. Tap Frame to jump to it.

Try it

A working prototype of the main path. Tap a device, use the touchpad, hold the mic, open the ⋮ menu and switch to the D-pad, change the AC setpoint, open Device settings. Screens push and go back with the real transitions.

Visual system

Defined once at the top of this file as CSS variables, for light and dark. Swatches below are read live from the variables, so they follow the theme switch. Contrast ratios are computed from the same values (AA: 4.5 for text, 3 for controls and large text).

Start and Home

App start, loading, the first-run welcome, a full Home and the ways Home goes wrong.

TV remote

Google TV style: a bar with Back, name, power, keyboard and ⋮; a flexible touchpad with a 44 px dot; Back (64), Home (84) and a 64 px ink mic below. Works the same for LG, Android TV / Google TV and Samsung.

Voice

Hold the mic: Android TV / Google TV boxes take native voice; LG and Samsung dictate on the phone and type into the TV's open search box; with neither, the mic is not drawn. The app shows "Listening…" while the finger is down and a single line under the keys when something goes wrong.

Air conditioner

One screen for every AC, over Matter or IR. The link only shows as one quiet line at the bottom.

Fan

Five tall speed bars you can hit without looking, then Boost, Sleep and Light, and a timer.

Add a TV

Kind, brand, find on the Wi-Fi, pair. LG and Samsung ask you to press Allow on the TV; Android TV shows a code to type. Every failure says what happened and what to do.

Add an AC or fan

Brand, then Matter (if the brand sells Matter models) or IR code testing. Name and room are the shared last step.

Add an AC over Matter

The camera is the main action: point it at the QR code and it locks on by itself. "Paste code" and "Enter code instead" are the fallbacks. Then three plain steps, never a bare spinner.

Air freshener

The Godrej aer Smart Matic, over Bluetooth, in the same style as the TV, AC and fan. A big Spray now, Auto spray, the dispenser's status and the schedules it holds. The dispenser takes one phone at a time, so the app connects for each action and lets go within seconds; between connections the screen shows what it last read and says when. Schedules are written to the dispenser and read back before the app says Saved.

Add an air freshener

Air freshener in the kind picker, then Bluetooth (turned on if it is off), a list of nearby devices in the order they were found, a blink to be sure it is the right one, the shared Name it step, and the home time zone the dispenser keeps its clock in.

Settings and sheets

Device settings is a bottom sheet from the ⋮ menu (and long-press on Home). Settings is a pushed screen from the gear.

Dark theme

The page switch above changes every phone. These are always dark, whatever the page setting, so the key screens can be compared at a glance.

Components and states

The same pieces as src/ui, in every state: default, pressed, disabled, loading, selected, error. Tap the pressed examples to feel the press.

Motion

Five durations and four easings, used everywhere. Nothing moves that does not explain a change: pushes show where you came from, sheets and menus grow from their trigger, toasts arrive from below, loops only run while something is genuinely in progress.

Token changes for the app

What this mock needs that src/theme/tokens.ts does not have yet. Everything else is the app's current value.

Copy changes for the app

Strings the mock shows that are not in src/i18n/en yet. Frames that use them carry a "copy not in app yet" tag.

  • Voice, sent (proposed): "Sent to the TV". The app has no sent or heard state after a voice message; this is the proposal (Voice section).
  • Common: common.tagline "Nothing leaves your home network." · common.openSettings "Open Settings" · common.saved "Saved".
  • Add a TV: addTv.pairedFirst "Paired" (addTv.paired stays "Paired again") · addTv.localNetworkOff (the line below).
  • Devices and TV: device.removed "Device removed" · ac.offHint "Tap power to turn on" · tv.inputNow "Now" · tv.voiceSent "Sent to the TV".
  • AC and fan labels are plain words (icons are drawn): "Cool", "Dry", "Fan", "Auto", "Swing", "Sleep", "Boost", "Light"; status.connected is "Connected · how it's connected" without the leading dot.
  • Air freshener: its copy is in the app now (src/i18n/en/aer.ts, aerAlerts.ts, and the add.* and settings.* keys), so its frames carry no tag.
  • Local network denied (iOS): "Local Network is off for One Remote. Turn it on in Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network." with an "Open Settings" button (Add TV section).
