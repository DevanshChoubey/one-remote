One Remote: mockup v3

One device, one card, one remote. How the app talks to a device (Wi-Fi, Matter or IR) is the app's problem, not a kind of device you pick. An AC is an AC: it gets the same screen whether it is reached over Matter or IR, and the app uses the best link that works right now.

Decided in v1 review
  • TV: touchpad by default, D-pad on choice (like the Google TV app).
  • AC: dial with + and − buttons.
  • Home: grouped by room.
  • One device, one card, one screen. The link (Wi-Fi, Matter, IR) is not a device type and is not shown in the main UI. On phones without IR, devices that only take IR are greyed out with the reason.
  • A device can have more than one link. A Matter AC that also has an IR remote keeps IR as a quiet backup, used when Matter or Wi-Fi can't be reached.
